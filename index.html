If they still don't pay

The landlord ignored the demand letter. The escalation path in Bengaluru, in order, with what each step costs and how long it usually takes.

1

Legal demand notice (₹0–3,500)

A lawyer's notice on letterhead, or the letter this tool drafted sent by registered post. Most disputes settle here — a large share of tenants who send a formal notice recover the full amount within weeks.

2

Police complaint (₹0)

Criminal breach of trust under Section 406 IPC applies when a landlord flatly refuses to return the deposit. A complaint at the local police station costs nothing and moves landlords who ignored the letter.

3

Civil suit for recovery

For significant amounts: file for recovery with interest and costs. Slow and formal, but the registered agreement and your evidence file are exactly what this stage needs.

4

Rental housing court / authority

Under the Model Tenancy Act framework, rent authorities adjudicate deposit disputes faster than civil courts. Check whether Karnataka's rules have been notified for your situation.
